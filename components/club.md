## Jatayu:
ABOUT CLUB
Project Jatayu, founded in 2009, is one of India’s
top student led teams shaping the future of
Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs). From our
debut at the Australian Outback Challenge to
competing on the global stage at the AUVSI’s
SUAS, we’ve grown into a powerhouse of
innovation. Bringing together talent from
Aerospace, Mechanical, Electronics, and
Computer Science, we design drones that merge
precision engineering with creative vision. Our
UAVs take on real world challenges in
surveillance, reconnaissance, and agriculture,
offering opportunities for innovators from
every branch of engineering.

## Garuda:

## Team Ashwa:
ABOUT CLUB
Ashwa Racing, India’s first Formula
Student team, has been driving
innovation since 2003. From building its
first prototype in 2005 to competing at
FSAE Australasia, FS Germany, and FS
Italy, the team has proudly represented
India on the global stage. In 2015, Ashwa
ventured into hybrid technology, earning
a world top-10 rank in its very first
attempt. Today, with over 20 years of
legacy, the team builds multiple
prototypes annually across combustion
and hybrid categories, backed by strong
industry collaborations and a vision to
shape the future of mobility.
